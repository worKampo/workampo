# ADR-0052: Only living specs in `master`, and flow by size

## Context

Adopted on 2026-11-12 (formerly proposal P-2). [ADR-0016](0016-procedure-harness.md), [ADR-0020](0020-ticket-lifecycle.md), [ADR-0024](0024-vision-domains-prds-and-tickets.md), [ADR-0034](0034-prds-slicing-and-ticket-format.md), [ADR-0036](0036-language-and-glossary.md), [ADR-0037](0037-roadmap-board-and-wip-limit.md), [ADR-0040](0040-process-metrics-and-monthly-review.md) and [ADR-0042](0042-traceability.md) are already aligned with it. Revised on 2026-11-14 with the three moments of [ADR-0020](0020-ticket-lifecycle.md): size no longer changes the person's points of intervention.

**Problem**: every ticket left a frozen folder in `master` (`intent.md`, `spec.md`, `plan.md`, `tasks.md`, `verification.md`) that goes out of date the next day and nobody rereads; and the cycle weighed the same for a text tweak as for the session.

## Decision

- **Permanent in `master`**: vision, living specs (`docs/domains/<domain>.md`), ADRs, `docs/design/`, `docs/platform.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `REVIEW.md`, roles, `GLOSSARY.md`, `docs/learnings.md` and product content (help, legal, changelog). `docs/prds/` and `docs/fixes/` do not exist.
- **Temporary**:

| Piece | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| PRD (with optional hypothesis) | Body of the `type:prd` issue |
| Ticket intent | Body of the ticket's issue |
| Rule change (change spec) | The examples are approved in the issue (moment 1, [ADR-0020](0020-ticket-lifecycle.md)); on the branch they are edited directly in the living spec and the PR diff is the change spec. The spec reviewer checks that it matches what was approved |
| `plan.md`, `tasks.md`, `PROGRESS.md` | Separate files on the branch; `pnpm work pr` deletes them when opening the PR, in the same commit; the plan becomes a **"Plan"** comment on the PR, the tasks stay in the issue's checklist and the state in the "Progress" comment ([ADR-0016](0016-procedure-harness.md)). No temporary file reaches `master` (CI rejects the PR if any exists) and no commit is needed after approval, which could invalidate it |
| Verification | "Verification" comment on the PR, generated by CI |

- **Marks in the living spec** (Gherkin tags):
  - `@wip(#n)` on new or changed rules while work is on the branch; it never reaches `master` (CI rejects it).
  - When the PR is opened it becomes `@issue(#n)`: **one per rule** (`Rule:` block), not per example; it indicates the last issue that changed it. Fine detail is given by `git log` and the trailers.
  - What is under construction across the whole product is seen on the board and in `pnpm work status`, not in `master`.
- **Examples with a stable identifier**: in the issue they are numbered locally (E1, E2…); `pnpm specs:ids` assigns the final one when they are written into the living spec, with format `<domain>-<issue>-<n>` (`@id:companies-15-1`), which never collides between parallel tickets. Each test cites the identifier of its example; CI fails if there are examples without a test or tests of examples that no longer exist, and with that it generates the verification matrix. Nobody writes it by hand.
- **Domains instead of areas**: the former areas mixed domains, a surface (`backoffice`), technical mechanisms (`flags`, `platform`) and content (`content`, `legal`), and that left rules with two possible homes. Living specs are grouped only by domain (a business context with its own rules and vocabulary):

| Living spec | Includes | Replaces |
|---|---|---|
| `access` | Login, session, one-time codes, staff access ("Enter as": reason, duration, audit) | `auth` and part of `backoffice` |
| `companies` | Company lifecycle, slug, settings, members and roles, invitations, ownership, deletion | `companies`, `members` and part of `backoffice` |
| `billing` | Plans, plan limits and flags, payments, grace, plan changes | `billing` and `flags` |
| `public-site` | Company landing: sections, publishing, SEO, the company's content | `landings` |

  - Outside the living specs: `backoffice` is not a domain (it applies rules of other domains; system health is operations); `platform` is operations documentation (`docs/platform.md` and runbooks); the `flags` mechanism is technical and what each plan allows goes in `billing`; `content` and `legal` are product content ([ADR-0049](0049-platform-content.md)), and whatever is a rule (e.g. data suppression, [ADR-0027](0027-legal-and-data-protection.md)) goes to the affected domain.
  - **Ownership rule**: a rule lives in the domain that owns the data or the decision, even if the screen is on another surface ("only an owner can delete the company" goes in `companies`). Test: "how does X work?" is answered with a single file.
  - **It grows by splitting**: when a domain exceeds about 300 lines it becomes a folder (`docs/domains/companies/team.md`, `invitations.md`…), when it hurts and not in advance.
  - **GitHub labels**: domains (`area:access`, `area:companies`, `area:billing`, `area:public-site`) have a living spec and the spec reviewer requires updating it if behaviour changes; `area:platform`, `area:content` and `area:backoffice` are support labels that only filter the board.
- **Living spec template**: Purpose · Rules (rules with Gherkin examples) · Terms (links to `GLOSSARY.md`) · Out of scope. Observable behaviour only: no tables, endpoints or libraries.
- **Publishing the living specs** (2026-11-13): for now they are shared with the GitHub link (the repo is public). When there are specs with real content and someone to show them to, a chore sets up the **living documentation**: a static website generated from `docs/domains/` (Astro + Starlight) with the test status of each example (by `@id`), published for free (GitHub Pages, or a Worker with Cloudflare Access if the repo goes private). Separate from the customer help ([ADR-0025](0025-user-help-with-starlight.md)) and always generated, never copied to another tool.
- **Ticket size** (proposed by the slicer, or by the agent in standalone tickets, and confirmed by the person in the issue):

| Size | Examples | Agent path | Automatic review |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S** | Bounded bug, text, visual tweak, hotfix | Moment 1 is the issue itself; issue → branch → PR, with the tasks as the issue's checklist; a bug starts with its regression test | CI, CodeRabbit and a reduced verification package from the verifier |
| **M** | Normal feature, without touching data model, API contracts or migrations (if it does, it becomes L) | No Design and no `plan.md` (the plan fits in `tasks.md`): approved examples → living spec and acceptance scenarios → tasks → PR | + test designer, protected scenarios (mandatory), spec reviewer and UI/UX if applicable |
| **L / critical zone** | Session, isolation, billing, migrations, secrets | The eleven steps of [ADR-0020](0020-ticket-lifecycle.md), with `plan.md` | + Security section and security reviewer, `max` level ([ADR-0022](0022-model-and-effort-by-complexity.md)) |

- **Hotfix**: follows the S path, but if it touches a critical-zone path it carries the L reviewers (test designer and security reviewer).

- **The person intervenes the same in all sizes**: the three moments of [ADR-0020](0020-ticket-lifecycle.md) (ask, check, publish). Size decides how much work and automatic review there is before moment 2, not how many times it is the person's turn.

- **How it is classified**: three questions in order; the first one that applies wins.
  1. Does it touch a critical zone (session, isolation, migrations, billing, secrets), the data model, an API contract or a migration? → **L**.
  2. Does it add or change business rules (Gherkin examples of the living spec) or introduce a new interface pattern or component (level 3, [ADR-0031](0031-ui-ux-designer.md))? → **M**.
  3. Otherwise → **S**: a bug that restores the behaviour the spec already describes, texts, visual tweaks, chores with no behaviour change.
  - Edge cases: a bug that reveals an unwritten rule is **M** (it must be added to the living spec). Many S changes together are still S: size measures risk and process, not hours.
- **When**: for the tickets of a PRD, in slicing (P2, [ADR-0034](0034-prds-slicing-and-ticket-format.md)); for standalone ones (bugs, chores), when the issue is created, with a one-line justification from the agent. The person confirms it: without a confirmed size the ticket does not move to Ready (Definition of Ready, [ADR-0034](0034-prds-slicing-and-ticket-format.md)) and `pnpm work start` does not start.
- **Where**: issue label `size:S`, `size:M` or `size:L`; it is seen and filtered on the board, and `pnpm work status` and CI read it.
- **If it changes along the way**:
  - **Raising** it can be done by the agent at any time: if it discovers the ticket touches something from question 1, it changes the label, notes it in "Decisions made" and follows the L path from the step it is at. It only stops if the reason is a mandatory stop of [ADR-0020](0020-ticket-lifecycle.md) (e.g. a critical zone the spec does not cover).
  - **Safety net in CI**: if the diff touches critical-zone paths (migrations, session, billing, secrets; list in `docs/procedures/critical-paths.md`) and the label is not `size:L`, the PR fails and asks for reclassification.
  - **Lowering** it is decided only by the person.

- **`plan.md` only in L and critical zones**; in S and M the plan fits in `tasks.md`.
- **`pnpm work` commands** (one script with subcommands in `scripts/work/`; the person and the agents use them alike; a Claude Code `/work` only forwards to them):

| Command | What it does | Changes something |
|---|---|---|
| `pnpm work` | Help: available actions and, on a ticket branch, the suggested next step | No |
| `pnpm work status [#n…]` | Without numbers: everything in progress (branches with `PROGRESS.md`, open PRs, issues in progress), one line per issue with size, step, tasks and next. With numbers: the detail (tasks, open questions, CI, links). `--json` for agents and CI | No |
| `pnpm work resume [#n]` | Resume: without a number, lets you choose among what is half done; checks there are no unsaved changes, switches branch and prints the summary in the reading order of [ADR-0016](0016-procedure-harness.md) | Switches branch |
| `pnpm work start #n` | Creates the branch, `PROGRESS.md` and the issue's checklist | Yes |
| `pnpm work inbox` | Only what waits for the person: examples to approve (moment 1), PRs with a ready verification package (moment 2), paused production deployments (moment 3) and open mandatory stops, each with its link. `--json` for other scripts | No |
| `pnpm work verify #n` | Moment 2 ([ADR-0020](0020-ticket-lifecycle.md)): creates or updates the branch's worktree, resets the local database with the seed, starts the app, opens the browser and prints the test list of the verification package | Only locally (worktree and local database) |
| `pnpm work pr` | Checks `pnpm check`, turns `@wip` into `@issue`, publishes the "Plan" comment if there is a `plan.md`, deletes the temporary files, commits, pushes and opens the PR with the body filled in | Yes |
| `pnpm work close` | Closes the issue without asking; the agent proposes the learning, if any, in the closing comment | Yes |

  - `status` does not create another source of truth: it only reads `PROGRESS.md`, the "Progress" comment, the issue's checklist and the board.
  - Common rules: repeatable without duplicating anything if cut halfway, `--dry-run`, failures with a clear message, no credentials of their own (they use the `git` and `gh` session, [ADR-0041](0041-agent-boundaries.md)).
  - No commands to move cards or edit tasks by hand: `start`, `pr` and `close` do that, and the deployment workflow moves the cards to Staging, Release and Done ([ADR-0037](0037-roadmap-board-and-wip-limit.md)).
  - They are built in chore 5 of "Working foundation" together with `specs:ids`, `plan:check`, `plan:sync`, `db:seed`, `db:backfill` and the CI check of the acceptance scenarios (commit order and `/reopen-acceptance`, [ADR-0030](0030-test-designer.md)).
- **Learnings** in `docs/learnings.md` (in English, in `master`):
  - `AGENTS.md` requires reading the general section and that of each domain the ticket touches.
  - Fixed format of 1–3 lines: when it applies · rule · why · link to the PR or issue.
  - They enter when closing a ticket (proposed in the closing comment), when an error repeats or after a postmortem. The agent adds the learning to the next PR it opens, and the person sees it at their moment 2; if there is none within a week, it opens a small `docs:` PR.
  - They leave when they become an automatic check (lint, test, CI check) or a line of `AGENTS.md`.
  - Limit of about 100 lines: if exceeded, it is pruned or converted.
- **Outcomes (outcome-driven, minimal version)**: the PRD may carry a hypothesis in its issue; if it does, an "Outcome review" comment is added when it is closed ([ADR-0034](0034-prds-slicing-and-ticket-format.md)). None of this goes into the living spec.
- **Ritual**: 15-minute monthly review ([ADR-0040](0040-process-metrics-and-monthly-review.md)): automatic metrics from GitHub and CI, pruning of `learnings.md` and roadmap.
- **Traceability** ([ADR-0042](0042-traceability.md)): it relies on issue → branch → PR, the trailers and the `@issue` tags; there is no frontmatter of tickets or PRDs to validate.

## Consequences

Ruled out for now: a per-ticket signal, a separate outcome-review step, a retirement criterion and experiments with flags.

## Updates

- **2026-11-18** (decided by the person): the temporaries (`PROGRESS.md`, `plan.md`, `tasks.md`) live in `.work/`; the CI rule becomes "no `.work/` in what reaches `master`". `GLOSSARY.md` lives at `docs/glossary.md` and the review policy at `docs/process/review.md`.
